# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Garrison Honors Center (GHC) staff, directors, and students who reference daily operational to-dos and update operational processes.

## Product Purpose
A clean, desktop-grade workspace tool to view, search, and edit the GHC Operations Manual and Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) directly on the local disk.

## Brand Personality
Apple-like: ultra-minimalist, sleek, borderless, typography-focused, using warm amber selections and crisp, high-contrast layouts.

## Anti-references
- Cluttered, multi-level nesting trees that overwhelm document reading.
- Aggressive dark mode designs or high-saturation colors.
- Complex dialog windows, popups, and nested settings modals.

## Design Principles
- **Restraint Over Clutter**: Keep controls tucked away inside a simple toolbar and single sidebar to focus entirely on writing and reading content.
- **Aesthetic Craft**: Use system typography, warm amber accents, and clean borders rather than saturated fills or excessive rounding.
- **Seamless Local Sync**: Ensure edits auto-save to disk instantly with minimal latency or friction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast text styles to ensure optimal readability for long-form SOP reading.
- Seamless keyboard navigation shortcuts for writing text and toggling search.
