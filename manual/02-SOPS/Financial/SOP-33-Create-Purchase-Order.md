# Create Purchase Order

Use this SOP to begin an approved purchase for GHC, the Honors Program, or an honor society.

## Process

1. Confirm the purchase is approved under the budget or has special approval.
2. Obtain the Purchase Order Request form from office forms or the Bursar's Office.
3. Complete vendor, item, amount, account, and purpose fields.
4. Attach quote, invoice, or supporting documentation.
5. Route for required approvals.
6. Submit to the Bursar's Office.
7. Track the issued purchase order number.
8. Match later invoices to the purchase order.
