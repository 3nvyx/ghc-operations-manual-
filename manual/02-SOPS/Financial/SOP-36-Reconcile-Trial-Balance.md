# Reconcile Trial Balance

Use this SOP to reconcile Detailed Trial Balances against GHC and society financial records.

## Process

1. Obtain the Detailed Trial Balance from the Bursar's Office.
2. Compare transactions to invoices, receipts, deposits, purchase orders, reimbursements, and transfers.
3. Identify unresolved or unfamiliar transactions.
4. Confirm program payments are allocated to the correct societies.
5. Confirm scholarship, convenience fee, and outside-fund transfers are recorded.
6. Resolve discrepancies with the Bursar's Office.
7. Save reconciliation notes.

## Year-End Priority

June trial balances must be reconciled as part of annual closeout.
