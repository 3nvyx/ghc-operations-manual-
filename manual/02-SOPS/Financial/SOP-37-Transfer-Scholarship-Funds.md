# Transfer Scholarship Funds

Use this SOP when an honor society made scholarship awards available and funds must move from an operating account to a Foundation account.

## Process

1. Confirm the society scholarship award list and approved amounts.
2. Confirm the operating account has sufficient funds.
3. Coordinate with the OCC Foundation and Bursar's Office.
4. Prepare transfer documentation.
5. Route for required approvals.
6. Confirm the transfer posted.
7. Save documentation with scholarship and financial records.

## Timing

Source notes reference scholarship fund transfers around March or April and at annual closeout when awards were made available.
