# Verify Payment

Use this SOP to confirm whether a student's honor society membership fee has been paid personally or will be covered by a billable program.

## Accepted Payment Evidence

- OCC Tickets receipt for self-pay students.
- Application indication that the student is enrolled with a program that may pay the fee.
- Program authorization in the shared billing spreadsheet or by program contact confirmation.

## Process

1. Review the application packet for an OCC Tickets receipt or program-payment selection.
2. If the student is self-pay, verify the payment in OCC Tickets and record the amount and payment date in the Access society card.
3. If the student requests program billing, confirm the student appears on the appropriate shared billing spreadsheet or obtain authorization from the program contact.
4. If payment is missing and no program authorization exists, send the payment-required or missing-document follow-up.
5. Do not report the membership to the parent organization until payment or billing authorization is resolved.

## Notes

- Program policies vary by organization, membership level, semester, and regalia request.
- Students denied program payment should be advised promptly and given the opportunity to pay personally before the deadline.
