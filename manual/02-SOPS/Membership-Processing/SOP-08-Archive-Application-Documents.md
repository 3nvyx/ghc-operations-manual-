# Archive Application Documents

Application files should remain traceable after processing so staff can verify eligibility decisions, payment status, and communication history.

## Source Locations

- Outlook application folders.
- Google General Information Form responses.
- PDF applications and attachments.
- OCC Tickets receipts.
- Transcript review files.
- Shared billing spreadsheets.
- Access database records.

## Process

1. Confirm the application status is complete, missing documents, not eligible, withdrawn, or pending future review.
2. Ensure the Access record reflects the final status and relevant dates.
3. Move or label the Outlook application folder according to the office filing structure.
4. Keep transcript reviews and shared billing records in the appropriate Google Drive semester folder.
5. Retain payment evidence or billing authorization references.
6. If the application is pending because grades, coursework, documentation, or payment are missing, place it in the correct pending queue for follow-up.

## Filing Standard

Archive the documents so another staff member can reconstruct what was received, what was verified, what was entered, what was sent, and what remains unresolved.
