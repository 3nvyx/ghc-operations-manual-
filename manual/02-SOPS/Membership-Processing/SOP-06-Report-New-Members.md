# Report New Members

New members must be reported to parent organizations after eligibility, payment, and database records are complete.

## Reporting Timing

- Self-pay new members may be reported monthly, depending on the parent organization.
- Program-funded members are often reported at the end of the semester or when billing authorization is complete.
- PTK may receive eligible student lists for direct invitations and membership acceptance.
- Some organizations require annual reports in addition to routine member reporting.

## Process

1. Confirm the student's application is complete.
2. Confirm eligibility, membership level, payment or billing authorization, and welcome letter status.
3. Verify that Access fields needed for reporting are complete.
4. Prepare the parent organization report or portal entry.
5. Submit the report according to the parent organization's schedule and requirements.
6. Record the report date in Access.
7. Track or distribute membership materials when received.
