# Verify Program Billing

Use this SOP when a student indicates that EOPS/CARE, Student Equity, UMOJA, CalWORKS, Guardian Scholars, Global Engagement Center, Veterans Services, Pirate's Promise, Student Services, or another approved program may pay the membership fee.

## Process

1. Identify the program listed on the application.
2. Check the shared Google billing spreadsheet for the current academic year or semester.
3. Confirm the spreadsheet includes the student's name, ID number, email address, society, membership level, and fee amount.
4. Confirm the program has authorized payment for the specific student and fee.
5. If authorization is missing, request approval from the program contact before processing the membership as paid.
6. Record the billing status in the Access membership card or tracking notes.
7. Add the student to the program invoice list when semester or annual invoices are prepared.

## Program Policy Cautions

- EOPS/CARE generally excludes temporary and provisional membership levels.
- Student Equity may cover any level but has used a per-student semester limit.
- UMOJA and Global Engagement Center have generally covered one membership per student per semester.
- CalWORKS and Guardian Scholars have historically covered all available membership levels for their students.
- Veterans Services covers SALUTE memberships and handles SALUTE regalia separately.
- Pirate's Promise began covering one membership per student per semester in Spring 2025.

Always confirm the current semester policy before billing.
