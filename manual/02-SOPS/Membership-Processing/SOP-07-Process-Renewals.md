# Process Renewals

Renewals move students from an existing membership level toward the next eligible level when they continue to meet organization requirements.

## When to Send Renewal Reminders

Send reminders to students who:

- Are currently enrolled at OCC.
- Already hold a membership below Permanent level.
- May qualify to renew at the next available level.

## Process

1. Identify currently enrolled students with existing non-Permanent memberships.
2. Review eligibility criteria for the next membership level.
3. Confirm completed coursework, GPA, current enrollment, and standing requirements.
4. Request updated documents or payment if required.
5. Update the existing Access society card rather than creating a duplicate student record.
6. Enter the renewal start date, qualifying semester, payment or billing information, and welcome letter date.
7. Report renewal information to the parent organization if required.

## Notes

- Students should not be approved for a lower level if they qualify for a higher level.
- Permanent membership may trigger transcript notation review.
