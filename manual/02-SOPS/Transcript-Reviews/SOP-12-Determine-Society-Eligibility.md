# Determine Society Eligibility

Use this SOP to decide which society membership level a student qualifies for after transcript review.

## Process

1. Confirm the society's current eligibility criteria and membership levels.
2. Review completed coursework through the previous semester.
3. Confirm cumulative GPA and any qualifying-course GPA requirements.
4. Confirm the number of completed qualifying units.
5. Confirm whether coursework must be OCC-only or may include other institutions.
6. Confirm current OCC enrollment.
7. Confirm the student is in good standing.
8. Determine the single highest membership level for which the student qualifies.
9. Record the qualifying semester, qualifying units, GPA, and membership level in the review file and Access.

## Notes

- Students may not be approved for a lower level when they qualify for a higher level.
- Some societies require a minimum number of units currently in progress; if no minimum exists, enrollment of 0.5 unit or more may be acceptable.
- Permanent memberships may require Banner transcript notation.
