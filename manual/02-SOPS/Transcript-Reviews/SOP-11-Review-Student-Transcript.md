# Review Student Transcript

Use this SOP when no prepared transcript review exists or when a review must be verified against the student's submitted transcript.

## Review Criteria

Most societies start from these baseline requirements, with higher or more specific requirements depending on the organization:

- Minimum 3.0 cumulative OCC GPA, higher for some organizations.
- At least 12 completed college units with letter grades.
- Associate-level coursework when required, usually OCC courses numbered A100 or higher that apply toward an associate degree or transfer.
- Current enrollment in OCC classes.
- Good standing with the Dean of Student Services.

## Process

1. Confirm the transcript is complete enough for the society or program being reviewed.
2. Identify completed coursework through the previous semester.
3. Exclude in-progress coursework unless the society explicitly permits current coursework.
4. Determine whether coursework below A100 affects eligibility.
5. Calculate or confirm qualifying units and GPA.
6. Check whether external college or high school coursework is allowed for the specific organization.
7. Record the outcome in the transcript review file or the relevant Access fields.

## Caution

Eligibility invitations based on OIE data are estimates. A full transcript review may identify transfer coursework, grade changes, incomplete grades, second 8-week enrollment timing, or course-level issues that affect eligibility.
