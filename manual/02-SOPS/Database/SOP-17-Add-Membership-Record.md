# Add Membership Record

Use the relevant society or Honors Program card to add membership information after eligibility and payment or billing authorization are verified.

## Process

1. Open the student record in Access.
2. Select the button for the correct honor society or Honors Program.
3. Enter the approved membership level.
4. Enter the date all application materials were received as the membership start date.
5. Enter the previous semester as the qualifying semester for standard honor society applications.
6. Enter qualifying units and GPA from the transcript review.
7. Enter payment or program billing details.
8. Send and record the welcome letter.
9. Record parent organization reporting and transcript notation dates when completed.

## Caution

Use the correct card. A student's PTK record, AGS record, Honors Program record, and other society records are separate.
