# Create Semester Invoices

Use this SOP to invoice billable programs for approved honor society membership fees and regalia purchases.

## Before Creating Invoices

Confirm:

- Each student has a complete application.
- Eligibility has been verified.
- The program authorized payment.
- The shared billing spreadsheet is current.
- Any denied students have been notified and given self-pay instructions when appropriate.

## Process

1. Export or review the current semester shared billing spreadsheet.
2. Group students by billable program.
3. Confirm each student line includes name, ID number, email, society, level, fee, and approval status.
4. Separate membership fees from regalia charges when needed.
5. Prepare the invoice for each program.
6. Attach the supporting spreadsheet or student-level detail.
7. Send the invoice to the appropriate program contact.
8. Monitor receipt of payment.
9. Provide a breakdown of received payment by society to the Bursar's Office.
10. Update Access and finance tracking records.

## Notes

- Semester invoices may include multiple societies and multiple membership levels.
- Do not invoice a program for denied, withdrawn, unpaid, or unresolved applications.
