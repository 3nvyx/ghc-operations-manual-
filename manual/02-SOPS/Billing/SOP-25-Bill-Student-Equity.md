# Bill Student Equity

Use this SOP when billing Student Equity for approved honor society memberships or regalia.

## Policy Summary

Student Equity may cover any membership level, but source notes indicate a $200 per-student, per-semester limit. Regalia funding has often been unavailable, so confirm before billing.

## Process

1. Confirm the student is eligible for Student Equity payment.
2. Confirm the requested membership or regalia amount is within the current semester limit.
3. Add the student and fee detail to the shared billing spreadsheet.
4. Obtain or confirm Student Equity authorization.
5. Notify the student if any portion is denied or must be self-paid.
6. Include authorized charges on the semester invoice.
7. Record billing status in Access.
