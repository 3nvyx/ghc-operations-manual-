# Bill GEC

Use this SOP when billing the Global Engagement Center for approved student memberships.

## Policy Summary

GEC generally pays for one membership per semester per student. Source notes do not establish a consistent policy for specific membership levels or regalia.

## Process

1. Confirm the student is eligible for GEC payment.
2. Confirm whether the student has already used a GEC-paid membership in the current semester.
3. Add the student and charge to the shared billing spreadsheet.
4. Obtain GEC authorization.
5. Process only the authorized membership.
6. Confirm regalia separately before adding any regalia charge.
7. Record billing status in Access and include approved charges on the invoice.
