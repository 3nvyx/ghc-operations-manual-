# Bill CalWORKS

Use this SOP when billing CalWORKS for student memberships or regalia.

## Policy Summary

CalWORKS has traditionally paid for all available membership levels for its students each semester. It has also historically covered all available regalia for graduating students with Permanent memberships.

## Process

1. Confirm the student is eligible for CalWORKS payment.
2. Confirm membership level and any regalia request.
3. Add the student and charges to the shared billing spreadsheet.
4. Obtain or confirm CalWORKS authorization.
5. Process membership and regalia only after approval.
6. Include approved charges on the semester or annual invoice.
7. Record billing status in Access.
