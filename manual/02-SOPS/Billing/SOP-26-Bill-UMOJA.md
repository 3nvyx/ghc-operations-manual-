# Bill UMOJA

Use this SOP when billing UMOJA for an approved student membership.

## Policy Summary

UMOJA generally pays for one membership per semester per student. Source notes do not establish a consistent policy for specific membership levels or regalia.

## Process

1. Confirm the student is connected to UMOJA and requested program payment.
2. Confirm whether the student has already used a UMOJA-paid membership in the current semester.
3. Add the student and requested charge to the shared billing spreadsheet.
4. Obtain UMOJA authorization.
5. Process only the authorized membership.
6. Confirm regalia separately before adding any regalia charge.
7. Record billing status in Access and include approved charges on the invoice.
