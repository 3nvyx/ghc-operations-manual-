# Bill Veterans Services

Use this SOP when coordinating Veterans Services payment for SALUTE.

## Policy Summary

Veterans Services pays for Veterans Services students' SALUTE memberships only. Veterans Services acquires honors regalia and provides it free of charge to SALUTE members.

## Process

1. Confirm the student is connected to Veterans Services.
2. Confirm the requested membership is SALUTE.
3. Add the student and SALUTE fee to the shared billing spreadsheet if program billing is used.
4. Obtain Veterans Services authorization.
5. Do not bill Veterans Services for non-SALUTE societies unless a new written policy authorizes it.
6. Refer SALUTE regalia questions to Veterans Services.
7. Record billing status in Access.
