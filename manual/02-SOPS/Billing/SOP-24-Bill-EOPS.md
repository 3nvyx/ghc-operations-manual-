# Bill EOPS

Use this SOP when billing EOPS/CARE for approved student honor society memberships.

## Policy Summary

EOPS generally pays for memberships for its students except temporary and provisional levels. Common permissible levels include Initial, Associate, Continuing, and Permanent. Regalia coverage has varied, so confirm before billing.

## Process

1. Confirm the student selected EOPS/CARE or is listed by EOPS as eligible for payment.
2. Confirm the membership level is one EOPS will cover.
3. Add the student to the shared billing spreadsheet with name, ID, email, society, level, and fee amount.
4. Obtain or confirm EOPS authorization.
5. Process the membership as program-authorized only after approval.
6. Include the student on the semester or year invoice.
7. Record billing status in Access.
