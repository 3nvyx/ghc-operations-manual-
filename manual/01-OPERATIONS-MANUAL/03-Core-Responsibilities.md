# Core Responsibilities

GHC work extends beyond membership processing. The office supports academic recognition, records, finance, events, student leadership, transfer preparation, scholarship opportunities, and annual reporting.

## Membership and Recognition

1. Invite students who appear eligible for honor society or Honors Program opportunities.
2. Process applications, transcripts, payments, program billing requests, and missing-document follow-up.
3. Maintain Access database records for student information, memberships, payments, welcome letters, reporting, and transcript notations.
4. Report new members to parent organizations and distribute membership materials.
5. Review permanent memberships and honors graduation recognition when applicable.

## Program and Student Support

1. Support Honors Program admissions, tracking, transfer certifications, UCLA TAP, Honors to Honors, and graduation certification.
2. Notify students of scholarship opportunities, workshops, recommendations, and nominations.
3. Train student leaders and student assistants.
4. Advise or orient honor society advisors and attend meetings when needed.

## Finance, Events, and Operations

1. Prepare budget allocation requests and special funding requests.
2. Process purchase orders, vendor payments, reimbursements, invoices, deposits, trial balances, scholarship transfers, and closeout items.
3. Plan events such as open house, gala, receptions, recognition events, workshops, conferences, and student travel.
4. Maintain publications, applications, brochures, flyers, signs, and regular communication templates.
5. Track inventory for office supplies, regalia, society logo items, and event materials.
