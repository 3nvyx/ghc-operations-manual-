# Strategic Goals

GHC's strategic work is to make honors opportunities visible, usable, accurately recorded, and financially sustainable.

## Student Access

- Increase awareness among students who appear eligible for honors opportunities.
- Send invitations after grading periods and enrollment updates.
- Send renewal reminders to students with memberships below Permanent level when they are enrolled and may qualify for the next level.
- Reduce avoidable barriers by improving instructions, application forms, payment guidance, and missing-document follow-up.

## Operational Accuracy

- Improve eligibility review by documenting how OIE reports are filtered and how transcript reviews are completed.
- Keep Access records current for applications, payments, welcome letters, parent organization reports, and transcript notations.
- Maintain clear separation between qualifying semester and processing semester.
- Use "appears eligible" language until a full transcript review confirms eligibility.

## Sustainability

- Maintain accurate billing, trial balance reconciliation, scholarship transfers, deposits, and closeout documentation.
- Keep parent organization reporting current.
- Maintain inventory for regalia, society materials, publications, and event supplies.
- Preserve process knowledge so new staff and student assistants can complete recurring work with confidence.
