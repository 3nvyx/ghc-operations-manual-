# Mission Vision Purpose

## Mission

The Garrison Honors Center recognizes OCC students' academic success and future potential, and supports them in becoming their strongest academic selves.

## Vision

Every eligible OCC student should know about and have a practical path to use the benefits offered by at least one honors organization. Strong participation in honors organizations supports healthy treasuries, which in turn creates more opportunities for engagement, scholarships, recognition, and persistence toward degree attainment.

## Why Honors Matters

For students, honors recognition validates academic effort, builds confidence, and encourages continued achievement. Honors engagement also prepares students for university-level expectations through leadership, time management, conflict resolution, service, and collaboration with people from different backgrounds.

For families and supporters, honors recognition validates the support and sacrifices that make student success possible. Honors students can become role models for siblings, peers, and community members.

For OCC, honors programming strengthens the college's reputation for academic rigor, student support, character development, and community engagement.
