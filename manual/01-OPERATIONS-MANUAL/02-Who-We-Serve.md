# Who We Serve

GHC directly serves high-achieving OCC students, especially students preparing to transfer to selective colleges and universities in competitive majors. GHC also indirectly serves the broader campus by making high achievement visible and showing what is possible when students engage with academic opportunities.

## Primary Student Groups

- Honors Program applicants and members.
- Honor society applicants and members.
- Students eligible for honors recognition based on GPA, completed coursework, and current enrollment.
- Students seeking transfer preparation, scholarship opportunities, leadership experience, service opportunities, or graduation recognition.
- Students supported by billable programs such as EOPS/CARE, Student Equity, UMOJA, CalWORKS, Guardian Scholars, Global Engagement Center, Veterans Services, Pirate's Promise, and Student Services.

## Benefits of Honors Engagement

- Recognition for academic achievement.
- Preparation for transfer and university-level coursework.
- Leadership, service, and professional-development opportunities.
- Scholarship awareness, recommendations, nominations, and workshops.
- Graduation recognition through transcript notations, printed program identification, and approved regalia when applicable.
