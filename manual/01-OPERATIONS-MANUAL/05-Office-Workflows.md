# Office Workflows

GHC workflows usually begin in email, Google Drive, Access, Banner, OCC Tickets, or a campus partner request. The goal is to move each item from intake to verified record, then to reporting, billing, or follow-up as needed.

## Common Intake Queues

- Outlook application folders for honor society and Honors Program applications.
- Google General Information Form responses.
- Google transcript review request files.
- OCC Tickets payment records.
- Shared Google billing spreadsheets for program-funded memberships.
- OIE eligibility reports.
- Bursar and finance documents such as PORs, invoices, trial balances, and deposits.

## Processing Pattern

1. Confirm the request or application is complete.
2. Find or create the student record in Access.
3. Verify eligibility, payment, billing authorization, or other required documentation.
4. Enter the correct information in the relevant Access card or tracking sheet.
5. Send the appropriate welcome letter, missing-document email, ineligible notice, or billing follow-up.
6. Report the item to the parent organization, campus office, or billable program when required.
7. File or archive supporting documents in the correct Outlook or Google Drive location.

## Record Keeping Standard

Email alone is not the record. Final status should be reflected in the appropriate database field, Google sheet, Banner entry, finance document, or parent organization report.
