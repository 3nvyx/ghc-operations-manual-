# Annual Operations Overview

The annual cycle is complete when student recognition, membership processing, finance, reporting, inventory, staffing, and transition tasks are closed or carried forward deliberately.

## Year-End Completion Checklist

- BOT blanket approvals have been submitted and approved for student events, travel, and institutional memberships.
- Individual approvals have been requested and ratified for specific overnight or out-of-area trips when required.
- Travel reimbursement requests have been submitted and paid.
- Application forms and welcome letters are updated for the current academic year.
- Eligible-student notifications have been sent at least once per semester, with two to three sends preferred when possible.
- PTK-eligible student lists have been transmitted to PTK headquarters for direct invitations and membership acceptance.
- Received applications have been processed or set aside for the new year pending grades, coursework, documentation, or other requirements.
- Database entries for applications, payments, welcome letters, reports, and transcript notations are current.
- Financial records for each organization have been reconciled with June trial balances provided by the Bursar's Office in July.
- Scholarship funds have been transferred from operating accounts to Foundation accounts when societies made awards available.
- Purchase orders have been closed or the Bursar's Office has been advised that no further purchasing is anticipated.
- Vendor invoices and reimbursement requests have been completed and routed for payment.
- New memberships have been reported to parent organizations.
- Student membership fees owed to parent organizations have been calculated and processed.
- Membership materials have been received and prepared for student distribution.
- Billable programs have authorized payment or denied payment with reasons, and affected students have been notified.
- Semester or annual program invoices have been sent for membership fees and approved regalia purchases.
- Required annual reports have been submitted.
- Graduation lists have been reviewed, coded for honors graduates, and submitted by Commencement Committee deadlines.
- Transfer certifications have been completed by February deadlines.
- Student assistants have been interviewed, hired, and trained.
- Program review, interim updates, annual staff evaluation, mandatory training, and weekly Dean reports have been completed.
- Inventory has been reviewed for regalia, society logo items, office supplies, and needed replenishment orders.
