# Billable Programs

Several OCC programs may pay honor society membership fees or graduation regalia costs for eligible students. Always confirm current authorization before processing a membership as program-paid.

## Shared Billing Record

Maintain a shared Google spreadsheet for the academic year or semester. Give appropriate access to each program contact. Include:

- Student name.
- Student ID number.
- Student email address.
- Society or societies.
- Membership level.
- Fee amount.
- Regalia amount, when applicable.
- Program authorization status.
- Notes about denial, partial approval, or student self-pay follow-up.

## Common Billable Programs

- EOPS/CARE
- Student Equity
- UMOJA
- CalWORKS
- Guardian Scholars
- Global Engagement Center
- Veterans Services
- Pirate's Promise
- Student Services, when applicable

## End-of-Semester Process

1. Confirm all program-authorized applications have been processed.
2. Confirm denied students were notified and given the option to self-pay when appropriate.
3. Prepare invoices for semester or year membership fees and approved regalia purchases.
4. Attach or reference the supporting student spreadsheet.
5. Monitor payment receipt.
6. Provide a breakdown of payment by society to the Bursar's Office.
7. Update Access and financial tracking records.
