# Purchase Orders

Purchase orders support approved purchases for the Honors Program, GHC, and honor societies.

## Source Forms

Use the Purchase Order Request form from the office forms folder or the Bursar's Office forms/resources page.

## Routing From Source Notes

- Honors Program: Dean or designated approver > ASOCC or Student Life approval path > Bursar's Office.
- Societies: Dean or designated approver > Bursar's Office.

Confirm current routing before submission.

## Process

1. Confirm the purchase is approved under the budget or has special approval.
2. Complete the Purchase Order Request form.
3. Attach required documentation, quote, invoice, or vendor information.
4. Route for required signatures and approvals.
5. Submit to the Bursar's Office.
6. Track the issued purchase order.
7. Match delivered goods or services to invoices.
8. At year-end, close out purchase orders when goods have been invoiced or no further purchasing is expected.
