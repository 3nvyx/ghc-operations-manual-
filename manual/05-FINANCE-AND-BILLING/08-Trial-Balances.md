# Trial Balances

Detailed Trial Balances are used to reconcile financial records for GHC-related organizations.

## Timing

The Bursar's Office typically provides Detailed Trial Balances monthly, often around the 15th to 20th of the month. June trial balances are especially important for annual closeout and are provided in July.

## Process

1. Obtain the Detailed Trial Balance for each organization.
2. Compare transactions against office records, invoices, receipts, deposits, transfers, and reimbursements.
3. Identify missing deposits, unpaid invoices, open purchase orders, or unclear transfers.
4. Confirm program payments and society breakdowns have been recorded correctly.
5. Resolve discrepancies with the Bursar's Office.
6. Save reconciliation notes with the finance records.

## Year-End Standard

Financial records for each organization should be reconciled with June trial balances before final closeout.
