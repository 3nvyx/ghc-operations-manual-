# Key Contacts

These contacts and offices appear throughout GHC operations. Confirm current names and routing before sending approvals, billing requests, or reports.

## GHC Oversight

- Dean of the Library and Learning Resources: oversight for GHC operations.
- Vice President of Instruction: Honors Program oversight, because the Honors Program is an instructional program.
- Honors Program Faculty Coordinator: Honors Program coordination and advisory committee work.
- Vice President of Student Services: honor societies as student organizations.
- Director of Student Life: Honors Program allocated funds and honor society student organization processes.

## Campus Partners

- Institutional Effectiveness: eligibility and enrollment data reports.
- Graduation Office: graduation lists, transcript evaluation questions, and honors graduation review.
- Student Records and Faculty Services: transcript notation or record issues.
- Bursar's Office: purchase orders, trial balances, deposits, payments, cash boxes, and regalia sales.
- Associated Students and Fiscal Affairs: budget allocation and case-by-case funding approvals.
- OCC Foundation: scholarship awards, scholarship transfers, and award lists.
- Enrollment Center: student enrollment and record coordination.
- MESA: STEM travel and student resource coordination when applicable.
