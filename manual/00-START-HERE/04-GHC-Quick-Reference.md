# GHC Quick Reference

This quick reference summarizes the recurring GHC work most likely to require cross-checking.

## Core Systems

- **Access database:** Student records, society membership cards, Honors Program records, payments, welcome letters, parent organization reporting, and transcript notation tracking.
- **Google Drive:** General Information Form responses, transcript review request files, shared billing spreadsheets, and review workbooks.
- **Outlook:** Application intake folders, missing-document communication, welcome letters, and processing queues.
- **OCC Tickets:** Student-paid membership fee receipts and payment confirmation.
- **Banner 9:** Transcript notations, especially through SHATCMT.
- **OIE reports:** Eligibility and enrollment reports used to invite students or prepare transcript review work.

## Recurring Work

- Invite eligible students after grading periods and current-enrollment updates.
- Process applications, payments, transcript reviews, and welcome letters.
- Maintain Access database entries for new applications, renewals, payments, welcome letters, parent organization reporting, and transcript notations.
- Report new members to parent organizations on the required schedule.
- Maintain shared billing spreadsheets and invoice billable programs.
- Reconcile trial balances and close purchase orders.
- Coordinate events, leadership training, scholarship opportunities, travel, and graduation recognition.
- Complete annual closeout, audits, reports, and transition tasks.

## Important Cautions

- Eligibility invitations should say a student appears eligible unless a full transcript evaluation has been completed.
- Honor society eligibility is usually based on coursework completed through the previous semester.
- Permanent memberships may require transcript notation review and Banner entry.
- Program payment policies vary by program and semester. Confirm authorization before billing.
