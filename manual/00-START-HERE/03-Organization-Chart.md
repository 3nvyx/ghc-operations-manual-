# Organization Chart

GHC work sits at the intersection of instructional programming, student organizations, finance, student services, and external honor society partners.

## Oversight and Authorization

1. **Dean of the Library and Learning Resources:** GHC operational oversight.
2. **Vice President of Instruction:** Honors Program oversight as an instructional program.
3. **Honors Program Faculty Coordinator:** Honors Program coordination and advisory committee connection.
4. **Vice President of Student Services:** Honor societies as student organizations.
5. **Director of Student Life:** Honors Program allocated funds and honor society student organization processes.

## Operational Partners

- Institutional Effectiveness: eligibility reports and data support.
- Bursar's Office: purchase orders, payments, deposits, trial balances, and cash handling.
- Graduation Office, Student Records, and Faculty Services: transcript, graduation, and notation coordination.
- Associated Students and Fiscal Affairs: funding and expenditure approvals.
- OCC Foundation: scholarship funds and awards.
- Billable programs: authorization and payment of eligible student membership fees and regalia.

## External Partners

GHC also works with honor society parent organizations, conference hosts, and transfer partners including HTCC, WRHC, NCHC, Bay Honors Consortium, UCLA/TAP, and UCI Honors to Honors.
