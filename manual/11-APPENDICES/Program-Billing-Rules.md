# Program Billing Rules

These rules summarize source notes for common program-funded memberships. Policies can change, so confirm current authorization each semester before billing.

## EOPS/CARE

- Pays for memberships for EOPS students except temporary and provisional levels.
- Permissible levels include Initial, Associate, Continuing, and Permanent.
- Regalia coverage has varied, so confirm current policy before billing.

## Student Equity

- May cover any level of membership.
- Has used a $200 per-student, per-semester limit.
- Regalia funding has not always been available.

## UMOJA

- Pays for one membership per semester per student.
- No consistent policy is documented for specific membership levels.
- Regalia history is limited, so confirm before billing.

## CalWORKS

- Has traditionally paid for all membership levels available to its students each semester.
- Has historically covered all available regalia for graduating students with Permanent memberships.

## Guardian Scholars

- Has traditionally paid for all membership levels available to its students each semester.
- Has historically covered all available regalia for graduating students with Permanent memberships.

## Global Engagement Center

- Pays for one membership per semester per student.
- No consistent policy is documented for specific membership levels.
- Regalia history is limited, so confirm before billing.

## Veterans Services

- Pays for Veterans Services students' SALUTE memberships only.
- Acquires honors regalia and provides it free of charge to SALUTE members.

## Pirate's Promise

- New as of Spring 2025: pays for one membership per student per semester.
- Confirm regalia policy with Student Services before billing.
